# Astrus

> Astrus is an AI-powered microchip design company automating the physical layout of analog
> circuits. Founded in 2022 by Brad Moon (CEO) and Zeyi Wang (CTO), based in Toronto and
> Waterloo, Canada, it applies deep reinforcement learning — AlphaGo-style self-play — to
> generate thousands of manufacturable analog layouts in seconds, targeting the high-speed
> SERDES interconnects used in data-center GPUs. Astrus raised $8M USD in seed funding led by
> Khosla Ventures.

## Status of the developer surface

Astrus publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-10 there is no developer portal, no
documentation site, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no CLI, no webhook catalog,
no MCP server, and no A2A agent card on any host Astrus controls. The company's product is a
private, authenticated web application (app.astrus.ai) reached through an AWS Cognito login;
access is not self-serve. The public website is a five-page Framer marketing site.
